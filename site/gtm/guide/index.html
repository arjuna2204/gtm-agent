Guide

How to use Pipeline Scout

Five minutes to set up, about 30 minutes a week to run. Everything below works in the browser with no account.

Quick start

  1. Open Find accounts and pick the starting template closest to what you sell, or choose Custom product.
  2. Edit the product and buyer so they match your real ideal customer, then press Find accounts. The list re-ranks instantly.
  3. Open the top accounts, check the sources, and press Write brief and emails to get a sourced draft you can edit and send.

Your setup is saved in this browser, so next time you open the app it's ready to go.

Describe your product

The product description does two jobs. The buying team decides which hiring counts as a signal. The name, one-liner and problems shape the drafted emails.

  • What you sell: a short category phrase, such as "an AP automation platform". It's used as "we sell…" in drafts.
  • One-line description: what it does and the outcome. Be specific, but don't include numbers you can't back up. The writer is told never to invent results.
  • Who usually buys it: 2 to 4 job titles. The draft picks one as the person to target.
  • Buying team: the team whose growth creates the need. Spend tools: Finance. Dev tools: Engineering and Data. Payroll: People & HR and Finance. Pick one or two; picking everything makes every company look like a fit.
  • Problems it solves: optional, but it makes drafts sharper. Use the words your customers use.
Too vague: "Software that helps businesses grow."
Better: "Automates invoice capture, approvals and vendor payments so finance teams stop keying bills by hand."

Choose your buyer

  • Company size: estimated from the number of open roles or, when there's no job board, from the size of the raise. Pick the bands you actually close; none selected means any size.
  • Raised money within: 90 days is a good default. New money gets spent fast, and the first 30 days are the strongest.
  • US states: leave blank for the whole US, or enter state codes like NY, NJ, CT for a territory.
  • Must be hiring on the buying team: turn this on for a short, high-intent list. Leave it off to also see companies that only just raised.

Work an account

  1. Check the sources. Click the filing, job post or article behind each signal. If a job is gone or the match looks wrong, move on.
  2. Write the brief and emails. The draft cites the signals it used. Anything it couldn't back up has already been removed.
  3. Find the person yourself. The draft names a job title. Look the person up on the company site or LinkedIn. Pipeline Scout doesn't collect personal contact details.
  4. Make it yours. Add the first name, one line only you could write (a mutual connection, a product detail), and your signature.
  5. Send from your own tools and log it in your CRM.

A 30-minute weekly routine

Data refreshes every morning, so a weekly rhythm keeps your list current without living in the tool.

Monday, 15 minOpen the app. Sort by score. Pick the 10 accounts you'll work this week: the best scores with signals from the last 30 days. Export the CSV for your CRM.
Monday, 15 minFor each of the 10: check sources, write the brief and emails, find the person, personalize, and send email 1.
Thursday, 5 minSend email 2 to anyone who hasn't replied. Check the app for big new signals (a raise this week) and add them to the list.
Next MondaySend email 3 (the close-the-loop note) to last week's list, then pick the next 10.

For a team: one person sets up the product and buyer and shares the link (see below). Split the top accounts by territory or alphabetically so nobody double-contacts.

Write-ups and API keys

Ranking and research are free and unlimited. AI write-ups are free up to a few a day per visitor, with a daily cap across the site. For unlimited write-ups, use your own Anthropic API key:

  1. Create an account at console.anthropic.com and add a small amount of credit under Billing. Each write-up costs a few cents.
  2. Open API Keys, create a key, and copy it. It starts with sk-ant-.
  3. Optional but smart: set a monthly spend limit under Limits.
  4. In Pipeline Scout, open any account, expand Use your own Anthropic API key, paste it, and press Use this key.

Your key stays in that browser tab only and is sent straight to Anthropic, never to this site's server. Closing the tab forgets it.

Share and export

  • Copy link to this search creates a link with your product and buyer setup built in. Teammates who open it see the same ranking.
  • Export CSV downloads every ranked account with its score and source links, ready to import into a CRM or spreadsheet.
  • Drafts you generate are saved in your browser, so reopening an account shows them again.

Data and limits

  • Raises: SEC Form D filings, which private US companies file within 15 days of a fundraise. Funds, real estate and raises under $1M are filtered out. Some companies never file, or file late.
  • Hiring: public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job boards, classified into 10 teams. Companies on other job systems won't show hiring signals. Roles count only if they were posted in the last 120 days, and stop counting three weeks after they leave the board.
  • New leaders: news headlines about new CFOs and VPs of Finance, each confirmed by Claude. Other kinds of leaders aren't tracked yet.
  • Estimates: company size and stage are estimates and are always labelled that way.
  • Coverage: US companies. This is a focused first pass, not a complete market map.

Outreach ground rules

Pipeline Scout drafts; you're the sender. A few basics keep outreach effective and on the right side of email rules like CAN-SPAM (this isn't legal advice):

  • Use honest subject lines and say who you are and where you work.
  • Include a simple way to opt out, and honor it.
  • Write to people about their role at their company, and keep the volume human. Don't blast the whole list.
  • Check every fact before you send it. The sources are one click away.

Run your own copy

The code is open at github.com/arjuna2204/gtm-agent. To run it for your team:

  1. Fork the repository on GitHub.
  2. In the fork's Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions, add SEC_USER_AGENT (your name and email, which the SEC requires) and ANTHROPIC_API_KEY.
  3. Edit config/profile.json to describe your product for the weekly pre-written write-ups, and data/seed_watchlist.csv to add companies you already target.
  4. In the Actions tab, enable workflows and run collect-signals once with 30 days, then weekly-score-and-write.
  5. Import the repository into Vercel and add ANTHROPIC_API_KEY there too if you want the free write-up button. The site is served from /gtm.
