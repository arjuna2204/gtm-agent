How it works

Companies announce when they're about to buy. Most reps never see it.

A fundraise, a new team lead, a burst of hiring: each one is public, and each one comes right before new tools get bought. Pipeline Scout watches for them so you don't have to.

1

Collect

Every morning it reads new SEC Form D filings (private fundraises), thousands of open roles on public job boards, and news about new finance leaders.

2

Describe

You say what you sell and who buys it: the team, the job titles, company size, region and how recent a raise should be.

3

Score

Every company gets a 100-point score for fit and timing, with the reason for each point, so you can see exactly why it ranks where it does.

4

Write

For any account, Claude drafts why now, who to target, a short brief and a 3-email sequence that cite only the signals on the page.

5

Check

Any sentence that can't be tied to a stored source is deleted before you see it. You review, edit and send from your own email.

Under the hood

Code does the scoring. AI only reads and writes.

Scores come from fixed rules, not a model's opinion, so they're consistent and explainable. Claude is used only to write, and the code throws out anything it can't back up with a stored filing, job post or article.

How it compares

Not a replacement for a full sales data platform. A sharper first pass.

Tools like Clay, Apollo, ZoomInfo and Common Room cover far more companies and contacts. Pipeline Scout does one job well: telling you who is about to buy, why, and with proof.

What's different

  • ✓Funding from the source. Fundraises come straight from SEC Form D filings, not a paid database.
  • ✓A source for every claim. Drafts can only cite stored signals, and anything else is deleted.
  • ✓Scores you can explain. Each point comes with its reason, so there's no black-box intent score.
  • ✓Built around the buying moment. It looks for the hires that come right before a team buys software.
  • ✓Free and open. Public data, open code, and your own API key if you want unlimited write-ups.

What it isn't

  • –Not a contact database. It names the job title to target, not a person's email or phone.
  • –Not complete coverage. Some companies never file a Form D or use other job boards, and they won't appear.
  • –Not a sender. It drafts emails; you review and send them yourself.
  • –Not exact on size. Company size and stage are estimates from open roles and raise size.
  • –Not connected to your CRM yet. Export a CSV and import it.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?SEC Form D filings from EDGAR, public Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby job boards, and news headlines. Everything is public. There's no LinkedIn scraping and no personal contact data.
How fresh is it?Signals are collected every morning. Roles that come off a job board stop counting after three weeks.
Does it send emails for me?No. It drafts them. You choose the person, edit the draft and send it from your own email or sequencing tool.
Can I use it for my product?Yes. Pick a starting template or describe your own product, choose the team that buys it, and the ranking updates instantly. The guide walks through it.
Is it free?Ranking and research are free. You get a few free AI write-ups a day; add your own Anthropic API key for unlimited ones. Your key stays in your browser.
What is it built with?Python and GitHub Actions for daily collection, plain JavaScript for the app, the Claude API for writing, and Vercel for hosting. The code is open on GitHub.
